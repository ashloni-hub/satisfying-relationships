Every strong ‘we’ starts with two people who know their own ‘I’

Most couples come in speaking only in “we.” We fight. We shut down. We can’t get past this. Doug’s approach starts by separating that “we” back into two people, each with their own patterns, their own triggers, and their own choices. Pressure in a relationship — over sex, money, betrayal, expectation — is inevitable. But once each partner can see their own part clearly, how they respond to that pressure stops being automatic. It becomes a choice. That’s where the real rebuilding starts.

How to Get Started

Here’s how Doug-on-Demand works, three simple steps.

  1. 01

    Reach Out Today

    Fill out the contact form with your details and a brief description of what prompted you to reach out. New clients complete a short set of intake forms online.

  2. 02

    Doug Calls You in 24 Hours

    Once your forms are in, Doug reaches out personally, by the end of the day, or within 24 hours if it is late or after hours.

  3. 03

    Start the Work

    Your time in therapy should match the work in front of you. Standard sessions run about an hour, with longer appointments available when a situation calls for more room to think or a hard decision to make.
