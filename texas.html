Couples & Individual Therapy, Statewide by Video

Online Therapy in Texas

Doug Lormand, LPC and AASECT-certified sex therapist, works with individuals and couples across Texas on sex, infidelity, and money, by secure video, with a first session booked in under 24 hours.

Therapy Across Texas, By Video

Doug is licensed to practice in Texas and sees clients online from Austin, Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, Fort Worth, El Paso, and everywhere in between. Sessions happen by secure video, so you meet from wherever is private for you, without a drive across town or a waiting room.

You do need to be physically located in Texas at the time of your session. Beyond that, where in the state you live does not change how the work happens.

Who This Is For

Texas clients reach out to Doug for a range of reasons, including:

  • A relationship that has stalled and needs an honest outside read.
  • Rebuilding trust and safety after an affair.
  • Low desire, mismatched libido, or other concerns in the bedroom.
  • Conflict over money, spending, or financial secrecy.
  • Wanting to talk to someone alone, without a partner in the room.

How to Get Started

Here’s how Doug-on-Demand works, three simple steps.

  1. 01

    Reach Out Today

    Fill out the contact form with your details and a brief description of what prompted you to reach out. New clients complete a short set of intake forms online.

  2. 02

    Doug Calls You in 24 Hours

    Once your forms are in, Doug reaches out personally, by the end of the day, or within 24 hours if it is late or after hours.

  3. 03

    Start the Work

    Your time in therapy should match the work in front of you. Standard sessions run about an hour, with longer appointments available when a situation calls for more room to think or a hard decision to make.

Texas Online Therapy FAQs

Do I have to be physically located in Texas?

Yes. Doug is licensed to practice in Texas, so sessions require you to be physically in the state at the time of your appointment.

Is therapy really online only?

Sessions are primarily held over secure video. That means no commute, and you can meet from wherever in Texas is most private for you. If you are interested in booking an in-person session, we can discuss that during our first video session.

What can we work on together?

Sex therapy, infidelity recovery, and money and relationship counseling, for couples and for individuals working through it on their own.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Reach out and Doug calls you back personally, usually within 24 hours, not weeks out.

How much does it cost, and do you take insurance?

Doug offers private-pay sessions and works with select insurance through third-party platforms, depending on what is available for your plan. He can also provide documentation for out-of-network reimbursement when it applies. You will get the specifics when you reach out.
